Cat Ring Twist

猫想从猫塔顶下来！如果你左右拖动并转动插在每层中的挂钩，你会发现一个完美契合的隐藏凹槽。当您匹配一层上的所有环时，该层就会解锁，猫会下一层。一旦你到达底部，小睡就是成功的——没有时间限制，没有失败。

🌀 60 个关卡和新元素 一起旋转的齿轮环、滑环、双环，甚至阴影凹槽——随着你的升级，新元素会出现。
🐾 猫指南 完成游戏并使用您收集的硬币来邀请新的猫来填充您的百科全书。当你陷入困境时，提示会告诉你家的位置！
🔌 离线 · 2 种语言 无需登录即可保存到您的设备。中文·英文。

Cat Ring Twist — a relaxed rotation puzzle with no timer and no failure. A cat sits on top of a cat tower: drag the rings on each floor left or right until their hidden grooves line up. When every ring of a floor is set, the floor comes off and the cat steps down; reach the ground for a nap. 60 levels with gear-linked rings, slippery rings, double rings and shadow grooves, plus a cat collection to fill with coins. No account — progress stays on your device. Free, ad-supported (banner, interstitial and rewarded ads through Unity LevelPlay, with a first-launch personalized-ads choice). No in-app purchases.
