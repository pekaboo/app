Yarn Tangle Cats

毛皮被猫塔别针缠住了！轻敲线末端的环，然后按下空的发光针以移动环。一根与其他线没有重叠的线被卷成一团纱并消失。如果您在移动次数内解决了所有线程，您就成功了。

🧶 60 个关卡和新元素 冰环、磁性别针、缠绕五个别针的线，甚至是相同颜色的双线——当你升级时，新元素就会出现。
🐾 纱球指南 打破木板并使用您收集的硬币来打开新的纱线并填充百科全书。当你遇到困难时，请使用猫剪！
🔌 离线 · 2 种语言 无需登录即可保存到您的设备。中文·英文。

Yarn Tangle Cats — a relaxed untangling puzzle. Yarn is tangled across the pegs of a cat tower: tap a yarn end, then a glowing free peg, to move the loop. A strand that no longer crosses any other winds up into a yarn ball. Free every strand within the move limit. 60 levels with iced loops, magnet pegs, king yarn and twin yarns, plus a yarn ball collection to fill with coins. No account — progress stays on your device. Free, ad-supported (banner, interstitial and rewarded ads through Unity LevelPlay, with a first-launch personalized-ads choice). No in-app purchases.
