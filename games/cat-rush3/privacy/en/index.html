3 Minute Nyan Nyang Dungeon Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 23, 2026

3 minute cat rush dungeon (CatRush 3)You can play without registering as a member. Both the Google Play version and the ONE store version use advertising services, and game progress information is stored in app-specific storage. Google Play Games login, leaderboard, and achievement integration are included only in the Google Play version.

1. Personal information processor and scope of application

TeamCP (hereinafter referred to as “the Developer”) applies this policy to the following two distributions of the Android game “3 Minute Meow Dungeon”:

  • Google Play version: com.teamcp.catrush3
  • ONE store version: com.teamcp.catrush3 (Same package name as Google Play version, separate signature)
  • Contact: teamcp.channel@gmail.com

The two distributions use the same package name, but have different signatures, so they cannot be installed together on the same device, and save data is not automatically linked to each other. The ONE store version does not include the Google Play Games SDK and the ONE store account/payment SDK. We do not operate our own membership accounts or game servers, and we do not directly ask for your name, contact information, email, photo, microphone, or precise GPS location.

2. Information we process and how we collect it

2.1 Saving game progress information and device

Game goods such as fish and souls, cat unlocking, artifacts and permanent enhancements, dungeon progress and statistics, local achievements and missions, language and sound settings, etc. are stored in the Android app-specific storage (Jetpack DataStore). We do not transmit this information to the developer's own servers. Access isolation of app-only storage is provided by Android and does not imply separate data encryption for the app itself.

The app allows Android backup. Depending on the backup settings of your device and account, the operating system or backup provider may back up and restore app data. Therefore, deleting an app does not guarantee that all backup copies will be deleted.

2.2 Advertising services

Both distributions use the same LevelPlay app settings (same ad configuration) as the Unity LevelPlay Mediation SDK, which can have ironSource Ads associated with them. The Unity Ads SDK and adapter are also included with the app. Actual advertising delivery and display will depend on advertising readiness and supplier response.

The ad format is a bottom banner, a full-page ad after a certain play completion cycle, and a reward-type ad selected by the user. Banners can be requested even while the screen is moving, and full-page advertisements can be requested without having to press a separate reward button. Rewarded advertising is selected by users for double result rewards, one resurrection per round, free relic box, etc., and rewards are paid only when a completion signal is received. If the ad is not ready or fails, you can continue playing the base game.

Advertising providers may process the following information for delivery, frequency control, fraud prevention, statistical and diagnostic purposes:

  • Advertising identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID, subject to device settings and availability), app and device identifiers
  • Device model, OS/app version, screen and language information, network type, IP address
  • Approximate location estimated by IP address, etc., ad request/impression/interaction/completion/error information

2.3 Google Play Games — Google Play version only

The Google Play version resets Google Play Games Services and may handle authentication status depending on your Google and device account settings. When users use leaderboards or achievement integrations, their public gamer name/profile, player identifier, highest stage reached, and achievement information may be processed through Google services. You can use basic single player and local history even if you are not logged in.

The ONE store version does not have the SDK, login, online leaderboard, or Google achievements integration, and local achievements and records are stored on the device.

3. Purpose of use of information

  • Save and restore game progress and settings, provide local records, achievements, and missions
  • Advertising transmission and measurement, compensation payment, frequency control, and prevention of fraudulent use
  • Google Play version of games leaderboard/achievement linkage and error diagnosis

5. Storage period and deletion

Device local data can be erased in Android Settings → Applications → 3 Minute Nyannyang Dungeon → Storage → Delete Data. When deleting an app, the app-specific storage is removed, but operating system backups may remain separately or be restored upon reinstallation, so please check the backup management of your device/account. Data for the Play version and ONE store version must be managed separately.

Storage and deletion of advertising-related data follows the policies of the relevant provider, such as Unity and ironSource. You can manage your Google Play Games data by managing connections in your Google Account or by going to “Delete your Play Games account and data” in the Play Games app. The ONE store version does not transfer new records to Google Games.

Children under 14 years of age and their legal representatives

  • Korea's Personal Information Protection Act stipulates that consent from a legal representative is required when processing personal information of children under the age of 14 based on consent (Article 22-2).

Since we do not differentiate processing methods based on age, the same processing under this policy also applies to users under 14 years of age. The app sends a ‘not directed to children’ signal on an app-by-app basis to advertising services, and this is not the result of verifying the age of individual users.
  • Personalized advertising: Personalized advertising is controlled by Android's advertising privacy/advertising ID settings. Users under the age of 14 should work with their guardians to delete their advertising ID from Android settings to prevent it from being used for personalized advertising.
  • Users under the age of 14 should not provide personal information such as name or contact information when reporting errors or making inquiries.
  • Rights of the legal representative and how to exercise them: The legal representative can request to view, correct, delete, or suspend processing of the child's personal information and withdraw consent. Measures to ensure the safety of personal information

  • Device storage: App history and settings are stored in the app-specific storage space that Android separates for each app, and are not sent to servers operated by TeamCP except as noted in this policy.
  • Transmission encryption: Information that the app sends to the Internet (ad requests, Google service integration) is transmitted through encrypted communication (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Minimum processing: Personal information stored by TeamCP is minimized by not receiving membership registration, password, name, or phone number.
  • Storage and Deletion: TeamCP does not have a server that receives game records, and the information on your device will remain on your device until you delete it.
  • Inquiry emails are deleted after the response is completed and are not kept longer than necessary. Personal information is stored only in electronic files and deleted at the end of the retention period.
  • Security measures for information processed by external businesses such as advertising and Google follow the policies of each business.
