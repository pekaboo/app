Cat Bento Sort

猫顾客订购了午餐盒！在旋转的零食传送带上，点击点击区域中的零食并将其放入配套的午餐盒中。如果您没有合适的容器，您将在存储槽中等待，但您先放入的零食将会消失。如果您在槽已满并且传送带被堵塞之前填写了所有订单，那么您就成功了。

🍱 60 个关卡和新元素 冰块、木桶、混合法术、更快的冲刺，甚至国王法术——随着你的升级，新元素会出现。
🐾宾客指南 邀请猫咪客人，并用打破木板收集的硬币填写插图书。当你陷入困境时，这就是猫的机会！
🔌 离线 · 2 种语言 无需登录即可保存到您的设备。中文·英文。

Cat Bento Sort — a relaxed sorting puzzle. Snacks ride around a looping belt; tap the ones passing the tap zone to drop them into the cat guests' bento orders. No matching bento? The snack waits in the holding slots, and the first one in leaves first — fill every order before the slots fill up and the belt jams. 60 levels with iced snacks, big bentos, mixed orders, rush and king orders, plus a guest book to fill with coins. No account — progress stays on your device. Free, ad-supported (banner, interstitial and rewarded ads through Unity LevelPlay, with a first-launch personalized-ads choice). No in-app purchases.
