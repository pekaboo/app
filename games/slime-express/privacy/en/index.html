Slime Courier Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 23, 2026

You can play Slime Delivery without registering. High scores, stars, skins, missions, and settings are saved to your device by default. The Google Play version and One Store version provide banner ads and optional reward ads. Google Play Games login and online leaderboards are only available on Google Play.

1. Personal information processor and scope of application

TeamCP (hereinafter “Developer”) applies this policy regarding the processing of personal information in the Android app “Slime Express”. The package name of the Google Play version is com.teamcp.slimeexpress, the package name of the One Store version is com.teamcp.slimeexpress.onestoreno see. Game history for both installations is stored separately in each app's device storage.

The app does not provide its own sign-up function where you directly enter your name, email address, or phone number, and does not collect or store your Google account password. Starting from ONE store version 1.2.0, ONE store in-app payment SDK (ONE store IAP SDK) is included for selling ad-free products (see 2.4). There is no One Store account linking function, and the app does not directly receive One Store member information or payment method information such as card or account. One Store's policies apply to One Store's processing of membership, installation, and download information.

2. Information we process

2.1 Information stored on the device

  • Highest score, stars held, unlocks/selected skins
  • Today's mission progress status and reward receipt record
  • App settings such as audio and language and number of game completions

This information is stored in app-specific storage on your Android device to provide app functionality and restore saved records. The position or remaining time of a game in progress is not saved, so when you run the app again, you will not continue playing that game. We do not transmit play records to our own game servers or external analysis servers.

The app allows Android automatic backups, so app data may be backed up or restored upon reinstallation, depending on the operating system/device and the user's backup settings. Backups provided by the operating system are subject to the policies and settings of the backup service.

2.2 When connecting Google Play Games — Google Play version only

On Google Play, when a user chooses to connect to Google Play Games, Google can process their authentication status, player identifier, and display name, and their best scores can be submitted to a leaderboard. Login is not required when starting the app, and single play is possible even without connection. The One Store version does not have this function and does not transmit Google Play Games authentication, player information, or leaderboard scores.

  • Google account verification status and player identifier
  • Submit your highest score to the leaderboard

2.3 Ad provision process — Common to Google Play version and One Store version

For both distributions, Unity LevelPlay brokers advertising, and the associated Unity Ads·ironSource Ads can serve banners and optional rewarded ads. Because the banner requests to be permanently displayed at the bottom of the game screen, ad requests and related information processing may occur even before you choose to view a rewarded ad. The banner may not appear depending on ad availability or connection status. Watching rewarded ads is optional for extra points, and no interstitial ads will be shown immediately after the game ends. Advertisers may process the following information to deliver advertisements, limit frequency, measure performance, prevent fraud, and diagnose errors.

  • IP address, device model, operating system/app version, language, and network connection information
  • Device identifying information, such as advertising identifiers (where permitted by device settings and applicable laws)
  • Ad request, exposure, click, and viewing completion status and error and diagnosis information
  • Approximate local information estimated based on IP address, etc.

The actual scope of processing may vary depending on the region of use, Android privacy settings, and the policies of each advertising provider. Configure the game so that even if ads fail to load, game progress is not blocked.

2.4 One Store in-app payment — From One Store version 1.2.0

In the One Store version remove ads I have a product (Product ID slime.remove_ads, a non-consumable product that stays once purchased). If you purchase, we will no longer request or display bottom banner ads, and you will still have access to optional reward ads for extra points (there are no interstitial ads in this game). Click ‘Restore Purchase’ in the store with the same One Store account to reconfirm the purchase.

Payment and payment method information processing is performed by One Store Co., Ltd. on the One Store payment screen. The app receives the following information from the device required to confirm the purchase from the One Store In-App Payment SDK.

  • Product ID, order number, purchase token, purchase status/purchase acknowledgment, purchase data and signature

The app uses this information to verify the signature on the device, sends the purchase confirmation to One Store, and stores only the product ID in the app's dedicated storage. We do not send your purchase information to TeamCP servers. Storage of payment and refund records follows One Store’s policies and related laws.

3. Purpose of use

  • Save game progress, settings, skins, missions and high scores
  • Provides user-selected Google Play Games leaderboard on Google Play version
  • Providing banners and optional reward advertisements, measuring performance and preventing fraudulent use
  • Diagnose service errors and maintain security
  • Confirmation and restoration of purchase of One Store version ad-free products

5. Storage and deletion

  • Device storage information: You can remove it from your device by deleting the app or deleting the app for that distribution in Android Settings. Operating system backups must be managed separately in the settings of the backup service, and if restore is turned on, the records may reappear upon reinstallation.
  • Google Play Games save information (Play version only): You can manage it through the Play Games data management feature in your Google Account and are subject to Google's retention policies.
  • Advertising service processing information: The storage and deletion policies of the relevant advertising business, such as Unity, apply. Unity informs you that it will be kept for the period necessary for the purpose of service provision, legal obligations, and security/dispute response, and this is distinguished from information for which developers can set a lump-sum retention period or delete it themselves.

6. User choice and rights

  • The Google Play version allows single play without connecting to Google Play Games, and the One Store version can be played without the connection function.
  • Watching rewarded ads is optional, and not watching them will not limit your basic play.
  • You can control personalized ads in Android's Ad Privacy & Advertising ID settings. If a privacy icon is provided in your Unity ad, you can use that icon to learn how to select personalized advertising and request information to be viewed or deleted. Limiting personalized ads does not stop all ads themselves, including banners.
  • If you need to confirm, correct, or delete information, you may request it through the contact information below.

7. Children’s personal information

Slime Delivery is not a service designed exclusively for children, and the store's current target audience is ages 13 and older. Content age ratings do not indicate that the service is directed to children. If we learn that the developer has directly collected children's personal information without the consent of the legal representative, we will take necessary measures, such as deleting the relevant information.

8. Ensuring safety

Your app's local data is stored in storage dedicated to Android apps. Google Play Games and advertising services that require network transmission are subject to each provider's secure communication methods.

Children under 14 years of age and their legal representatives

  • Korea's Personal Information Protection Act stipulates that consent from a legal representative is required when processing personal information of children under the age of 14 based on consent (Article 22-2).

  • Users under the age of 14 should not provide personal information such as name or contact information when reporting errors or making inquiries.
  • Rights of the legal representative and how to exercise them: The legal representative can request to view, correct, delete, or suspend processing of the child's personal information and withdraw consent. The method and processing period are the same as ‘Rights of information subjects and how to exercise them’ above. We only ask for the minimum amount of information needed to verify that you are the guardian and delete it once verification is complete.
  • If we become aware that the personal information of a child under the age of 14 has been processed without the consent of the legal representative, TeamCP will delete the information stored by TeamCP without delay.

Measures to ensure the safety of personal information

  • Device storage: App history and settings are stored in the app-specific storage space that Android separates for each app, and are not sent to servers operated by TeamCP except as noted in this policy.
  • Transmission encryption: Information that the app sends to the Internet (ad requests, Google service integration) is transmitted through encrypted communication (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Minimum processing: Personal information stored by TeamCP is minimized by not receiving membership registration, password, name, or phone number.
  • Storage and Deletion: TeamCP does not have a server that receives game records, and the information on your device will remain on your device until you delete it.
  • Inquiry emails are deleted after the response is completed and are not kept longer than necessary. Personal information is stored only in electronic files and deleted at the end of the retention period.
  • Security measures for information processed by external businesses such as advertising and Google follow the policies of each business.
