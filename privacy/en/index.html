TeamCP Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 19, 2026

TeamCP develops and operates Android games. This document is a general guide to all TeamCP games and Google Play Games integration. Information processed may vary by game, so please check the detailed policies of each game as well.

1. Operator

Personal information processor: TeamCP

Contact us: teamcp.channel@gmail.com

TeamCP does not operate its own game account, and does not receive name, email, phone number, or payment information directly from the app.

2. Games provided

  • Cat Gomoku
  • Cat Detective
  • Slime Express
  • DEAD FREQUENCY
  • 3 Minute CatRush Dungeon (CatRush 3)

3. Commonly processed information

  • Save your device: Game progress, settings, rewards and unlock status are stored in the Android app-specific storage.
  • Google Play Games (optional): Your authentication information and some of your game progress may be processed by Google for ranking and saving functions, such as login, player identification, and high scores. It is configured so that single play is possible even without connection.
  • Advertisement: Some games offer banner or optional rewarded advertising with Unity LevelPlay and Unity Ads. Advertising businesses may process device/advertising identifiers, approximate regions, and advertising event information to provide, measure, and prevent fraudulent use of advertisements.

TeamCP does not sell user personal information and does not collect play records through its own analysis server.

4. External services

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Delete Google Play Games data
  • Unity Game User Privacy Policy

External service providers may store or process information outside of your country according to their own policies.

5. Storage, deletion and rights

Device information can be removed by deleting app data or deleting the app. Google Play Games and advertising information follows each operator's storage/deletion policy and rights exercise path. If confirmation, correction, or deletion is required, please provide the app name and request type to the inquiry address above.

6. Children

TeamCP games are not a service exclusively for children, and the store's target audience is those aged 13 or older. Content age rating does not imply eligibility for privacy consent.

7. Change

If processing items or linked services change, this page and the effective date of each game policy will be updated.
